
## Tickers cheat sheet

| Market | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| US stocks (NYSE/NASDAQ) | Just the ticker | `NVDA`, `AAPL`, `MSCI` |
| Singapore (SGX) | Ticker + .SI | `DBS.SI`, `OCBC.SI`, `MIT.SI` |
| Hong Kong (HKEX) | Number + .HK | `9988.HK`, `0700.HK` |
| UK (LSE) | Ticker + .L | `SHEL.L`, `HSBA.L` |
